# Anchor Web Launchpad

Build a modern, professional one-page marketing website for "Anchor Web", a solo web design and development business based in Adelaide, South Australia that builds websites for small local businesses and sole traders (tradies, cafes, hairdressers, consultants, retailers, etc).

Brand feel: Trustworthy, reliable, fast, and modern — this should feel like a business a busy local shop owner can hand their website to and stop worrying about it. Avoid anything gimmicky or "techy startup" — lean clean, confident, and human rather than corporate or flashy.

Color palette: Primary deep navy (#0F2A4A) as the dominant "solid, trustworthy" color, paired with a dark charcoal/graphite (#1A1A1A–#2D2D2D) for text and secondary UI elements. Accent color: warm teal (#14B8A6) used sparingly for CTAs, links, icons, and hover states to keep it feeling fast and modern rather than heavy. Background: off-white (#F8F9FA), not pure white, for a warmer feel. Avoid pure black — keep the dark tones navy/charcoal-based so it stays "solid" without feeling cold or corporate.

Design direction:

Clean, modern layout with generous whitespace, strong typography hierarchy

Rounded, friendly UI elements — not sharp/aggressive

Fast-loading feel: minimal clutter, no heavy unnecessary animation, snappy micro-interactions on buttons/hover states

Fully responsive, mobile-first (most of my clients will view this on their phone)

Sections to include:

Hero — headline focused on the outcome for the client (e.g. "A website your customers can trust — live in days, not months"), short supporting line, primary CTA button ("Get a Free Quote" or "Book a Free Chat"), secondary CTA ("See My Work")

Why Anchor Web — 3-4 short value props with icons: e.g. Built for local businesses, Fast turnaround, Fixed transparent pricing, Ongoing support after launch (no disappearing after payment)

Services — simple website builds, small business landing pages, website refreshes/redesigns, ongoing maintenance/updates — presented as simple cards, not overwhelming

How it works — simple 3-4 step process (e.g. Chat → Design → Build → Launch) to reduce uncertainty for non-technical business owners

Featured work / case study — a placeholder section styled to showcase 1 client project prominently (I have one real client website to feature), with room to add more later

Testimonial — a placeholder quote block styled nicely, ready for a real testimonial to be dropped in

About — short, personal, first-person bio section establishing it's a real local person (based in Adelaide) they can talk to directly, not a faceless agency — this builds trust

Contact / CTA — simple contact form (name, business name, email, phone, message) plus direct email/phone display, closing CTA like "Let's get your business online properly"

Footer — business name "Anchor Web", simple nav repeat, socials placeholder, "Adelaide, SA" location mention

Technical/content notes:

Use "Anchor Web" as the business name throughout, with a placeholder logo (just styled text/wordmark for now — I'll add a real logo image later, so keep the header logo area easy to swap)

Use realistic placeholder copy, not lorem ipsum — write it as if speaking directly to a small Adelaide business owner who is slightly overwhelmed by "getting a website done"

Structure it as one page with clear anchor-linked sections (Home → Why → Services → Work → About → Contact) for smooth-scroll navigation

Keep the codebase simple and editable — I'll be coming back to update copy, images, and branding myself as the business develops

No pricing table needed yet — just point to "Get a Free Quote"
